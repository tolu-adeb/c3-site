c3.lab

A research lab for NQ futures intraday alerts. Every rule is written as code, and every rule is tested before it is allowed to post.

What it does

  • Reads 1-minute NQ and ES structure
  • Scores each setup from 0 to 100
  • Posts alerts and tracks them as virtual trades

What it never does

Place orders. It is alert-only. Decisions come from fixed, deterministic rules, so the same market always gets the same answer.

Where it stands

In private testing. Results go on the track record after at least four weeks of forward testing. Join the waitlist to hear when alerts open.
