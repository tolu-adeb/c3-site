Process / read-only

A path from bars
to a record.

The engine makes decisions. The site shows their exported record. Follow the pipeline, then inspect its evidence and limits.

Results not published yet. This is a static process description.

Feed

Bars arrive from the engine’s configured data feed. This site receives exported observations; it does not fetch prices or run a live feed.

Read the pipeline as an ordered list

  1. Feed

    Bars arrive from the engine’s configured data feed. This site receives exported observations; it does not fetch prices or run a live feed.

    §3

  2. Structure

    The engine records swings, breaks and liquidity levels from closed bars. The site only displays the structure included in an export.

    §4

  3. Context

    A session has a recorded bias, trend and reference levels. Context is exported with the session so a replay can show what was known.

    §5

  4. Intraday

    The engine limits when candidates can arm. Exported windows describe those times; the browser does not decide whether a trade is allowed.

    §6

  5. Models

    A candidate belongs to a model. The NQ engine studies reversal and continuation; MNQ strategy descriptions and evidence are separate.

    §8–9

  6. Score

    The engine exports a candidate’s score. The dial is a display of that value, with a threshold only when one is exported.

    §10

  7. Gates

    Named checks record pass or fail. A replay shows their saved verdicts; none of those checks are evaluated on this site.

    §11

  8. Tracker

    A recorded alert can be followed as a virtual trade from entry to exit. Stops, targets, costs and losses remain part of the exported record.

    §12

  9. Alerts

    Alerts inform a person. This site does not submit orders or connect to an execution account.

    §13

  10. Ledger and storage

    Closed trades and session decisions are saved by c3.lab. Signed bundles give the site a versioned, one-way view of that record.

    §14

  11. Lab

    Ideas are judged in a search window and then a locked holdout. Rejections and failed holdouts remain visible alongside candidates.

    Research

  12. Site

    The Worker verifies the bundle and publishes a final pointer. Public pages read exported JSON and label its source, costs and sample.
